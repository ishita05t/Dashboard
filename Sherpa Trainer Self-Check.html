Quick fixes

Most reported issues this month

A call or another app interrupted my session

Sherpa pauses rather than stops. The clip resumes on the same session once the call ends.

  1. Reopen Sherpa and check the session banner says Resumed.
  2. If it says Ended early, the two parts upload as separate clips — report the session and support will merge them.
  3. Turn on Do Not Disturb before a session to avoid it.
Recording stopped midway

Usually battery optimisation closing the app in the background, or the Core dropping its Bluetooth pairing.

  1. Check the Core is above 15% battery — see Device details.
  2. Exclude Sherpa from battery optimisation in your phone settings.
  3. Re-pair the Core if the device log shows a dropped connection.
Session finished but the score is missing

Scoring needs a stored transcript. Clips under about two minutes, or with no clear speech, return no score — they show as Not scorable in your library rather than as a low score.

  1. Check the clip length in Session library.
  2. If the session was full length and still unscored, report it and it will be re-run.
Mic or notification permission issue

Sherpa needs the microphone to record and notifications to tell you when a score is ready.

  1. Open your phone settings, then Sherpa, then Permissions.
  2. Allow Microphone and Notifications.
  3. Reopen Sherpa — Device details should show both as granted.
